# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

This repository holds HEY Research Lab's disclosure policy, the hall of fame and the scripts that draw its pictures.
If you find a security issue in it, or in anything HEY Research Lab publishes, report it privately through GitHub's
"Report a vulnerability" (Security → Advisories) on this repository, or email hi@heyresearch.xyz with a subject
starting "Security". Do not open a public issue. We aim to acknowledge within 3 working days. There is no bug bounty.

Want us to review your project's code? See [Ask for a review](README.md#ask-for-a-review).
